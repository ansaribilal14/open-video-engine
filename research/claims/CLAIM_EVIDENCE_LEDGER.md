# CLAIM / EVIDENCE LEDGER

> Directive: for every important technical belief — CLAIM, EVIDENCE, SOURCE,
> COUNTER-EVIDENCE, CONFIDENCE, EXPERIMENT, RESULT, DECISION.
> This prevents architecture from being constructed from intuition.
> Confidence: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH. A claim may NOT become an ADR decision until
> confidence ≥ MEDIUM with at least one independent implementation reference, or an
> experiment has been run.
